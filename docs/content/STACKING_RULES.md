# PHB stacking rules

The generic RULES_EFFECTS phases and sum/highest/replace modes are interpreter facilities. They do not establish PHB rules. Require an explicit profile policy before combining uncertain effects.

| Case | Source-supported treatment | Source PDF |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Advantage/disadvantage | Same type does not stack; both cancel regardless of count |18|
| Proficiency on a roll | Add once; mounted Vehicles plus weapon training does not double it |18, 114, 115|
| Criticals | Double damage dice; add flat modifiers once |31|
| Mastered/Empowered Manifestation | Cannot both reduce same power cost |57|
| Empowering a power | Once per casting; specific apex exception must be scoped |57, 91|
| Rapid Feed / Extended Magazine | Explicit nonstacking; no blanket stacking across all upgrades |106|
| Breakdown duplicate | Repeated active result deals2d6 ignoringDT, no recursive breakdown |116|
| Exhaustion | Cumulative levels; removing a level removes unavailable higher-level effects |33|
| Signature Wargear | At most three active benefits; change at rest/downtime |124|

Technical deduplication applies once per source/acquisition/local-effect identity; replaying an event or traversing the same grant twice must not duplicate it. Different item instances remain different sources. This is not permission to stack two copies of an item or repeated Talents.

General same-source/different-source flat bonuses, multiple multipliers, repeated resistance, shields, temporary effects and equipment-granted duplicate benefits remain unresolved unless a specific source says otherwise. Missing policy returns unresolved. Replacing an upgraded numeric benefit needs an explicit replacement mapping; retaining its manual source text does not authorize summing both values. Multipliers require a reviewed rounding boundary. Never use priority or array order to invent precedence.
