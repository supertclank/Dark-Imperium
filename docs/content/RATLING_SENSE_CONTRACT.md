# Ratling sense and fixed-effect contract

PHB PDF 23 explicitly supplies 60 ft darkvision and says multiple darkvision grants use the longest range. Canonical effect target `sense.darkvision` therefore admits only automatic, constant, nonnegative, base effects with `stacking.mode=max`. Other imported stat targets retain their previous sum/replace subset. This does not resolve PHB-RULE-058. The v3 adapter displays the maximum certified range and Small size; visibility, light, smoke, solids and psychic darkness remain contextual manual adjudication.

Fixed species attribute increases are read from the certified path’s automatic canonical source effects. Human ability allocation still comes from its mandatory choice. Older snapshots do not opt into species composition and retain existing calculations.
