# Non-Human level-one certification — v4

Completed 23 October 2026. **READY_WITH_MANUAL_RULES for the explicitly listed branches**, not unrestricted species/class/package publication. No new game-design ruling was made. Existing Human advancement remains through level 3; non-Human paths added here are level 1 only.

1. **Ogryn certification.** Fixed Strength +2/Constitution +1, 30 ft, Medium; Massive Build adds 3 HP per level. Big Lad applies Dexterity −2 with minimum 6 after species adjustments and grants Athletics. Contextual larger-size rules and equipment-frame restrictions are retained; overlapping contexts remain manual under PHB-RULE-058. Brute Strength provides an authoritative 1d6 + Strength damage roll without a fake inventory item; attack eligibility remains manual. Simple Solutions has its source one-use/short-rest counter.
2. **Ogryn publication.** Penal Worlder or Militarum Regiment → Ogryn Skullbreaker 1, Twitchy. Ripper Gun or Ogryn Club, Ogryn Plate, one Frag Bomb, rations and lucky charm. Rare Hiveborn/Voidborn remain blocked. Slab Shield is not a published option. Flak Plate remains unresolved under PHB-RULE-030. No standard firearm is substituted for Ogryn equipment.
3. **Skitarii certification.** Dexterity or Intelligence +2, Constitution +1; mandatory Noospheric Focus and Machine Focus skill choices; 30 ft, no default darkvision. No Psychic Potential explicitly rejects Psyker and Touched by the Warp. Integrated Auspex is manual, once per short rest; Targeting Protocol is manual, PB uses per long rest (2 at the only published level). Cybernetic Maintenance, poison resistance and conditional saves remain manual.
4. **Skitarii publication.** Forge World → Skitarii Veteran 1, Twitchy. Lasgun, Laspistol, Taser Goad or Combat Blade, Flak Armour, Auspex, maintenance tools, two Frag Grenades. Militarum is LIMITED and unavailable without trusted approval. Galvanic Rifle/Radium Carbine retain their ambiguous slash-separated damage candidates; Carapace/Data-Jack alternatives remain outside this reviewed package.
5. **Kin certification.** Constitution +2 and Intelligence or Wisdom +1, 25 ft, Medium, 60 ft darkvision. Guild-Craft's complete source domain is recorded: Technology, Investigation, or a tool related to engineering/mining/weapons/armour/voidcraft. **Only the two named skill branches are executable in this release; the open tool domain is not fully implemented.** Dense Build, Void-Hardened and Stubborn Kin remain manual. Grudge Memory is once per long rest. Class and Origin skill choices, selected weapon-group/armour proficiencies and manual resource counters are mapped.
6. **Kin publication.** Mining Hold/Guild → Kin Armsman 1 or Hernkyn Prospector 1, Twitchy. Armsman: Ion Blaster, Autoch-Pattern Pistol, Kin Mesh or Hearthkyn Plate, Kin Power Axe or Concussion Hammer, two Frag Grenades, oath-token. Hernkyn: Ion Blaster, Autoch-Pattern Pistol, Knife, Prospector Rig, survey auspex, climbing kit, claim-marker. Magna-rail is explicitly GM_APPROVAL_REQUIRED; there is no self-service override. Other primary alternatives and the Vehicles proficiency branch remain unavailable.
7. **Aeldari certification.** Dexterity +2, Intelligence or Wisdom +1, 35 ft, Medium, 90 ft darkvision. Inhuman Grace requires Acrobatics or Stealth. Keen Senses grants Perception and adds exactly +1 when another selected source already grants it; a third duplicate does not add another bonus. This is not Expertise. Battle Trance and conditional saves/social effects remain manual, with one-use/short-rest tracking.
8. **Aeldari publication.** Craftworld/Outcast → Aeldari Outcast 1, Twitchy. Shuriken Pistol, Monomolecular Blade, Shuriken Catapult or Ranger Long Rifle, Aeldari Mesh or Ranger Weave, spirit-token and camo cloak. Armour conditionals are visible manual notes. Warlock is not published.
9. **Psychic blockers.** Psyker/Warlock lack certified canonical power pools, executable power choices/actions, Warp Pressure/Perils integration and complete selected-path certification. Skitarii additionally have an explicit species prohibition. Existing 52 power candidates, including 38 required for tier-1 pools, remain blocked; no non-PHB defaults are imported.
10. **Ratling Sharpshooter preparation.** A reproducible prepared-only package and three tests cover all 16 equipment combinations, source quantities, one equipped armour and no invented grenades. Long-las/heavy-clothing candidate bytes are preserved. Mandatory cook’s-utensils/thieves’-tools proficiency mapping and executable tool/package certification remain incomplete. The preparation does not publish Sharpshooter. PHB-RULE-011 applies to the selected later Expertise context, not level 1 by default.
11. **Equipment promoted.** Seventeen archived source candidates: Ogryn Club, Ripper Gun, Taser Goad, Ion Blaster, Autoch-Pattern Pistol, Kin Power Axe, Concussion Hammer, Shuriken Pistol, Shuriken Catapult, Ranger Long Rifle, Monomolecular Blade, Frag Bomb; Kin Mesh, Hearthkyn Plate, Prospector Rig, Aeldari Mesh, Ranger Weave. Original bytes and SHA-256 values are retained in `review/archive/nonhuman-v4` and `review/nonhuman-v4-promotions.json`. Auspex is a separately source-mapped starting utility. Canonical inventory now contains 1,005 records, with 288 candidates remaining.
12. **Package compiler.** New packages use the existing generic structured item/equip/choice compiler. Its existing optional/nested/narrative/proficiency behavior is retained; an explicit approval-required branch now fails closed. No species-specific package compiler was added. Actual published branches contain no invented narrative items or free-form approval flags.
13. **Generic engine and dice.** Snapshot-declared mechanics compose score adjustments/floors, HP per level, skill grants, duplicate-proficiency bonus, psychic prohibitions, contextual size, frame constraints and innate damage. No species-specific formula branch was added. Item requirements and reviewed fit scopes are checked on the server. Consumption is derived from canonical weapon modes instead of a Frag Grenade ID. Frag Bomb uses its own source-defined 4d6 piercing, 20-ft radius, full/half damage and manual prone/no-prone outcomes. Exact rational halves are displayed (browser example 15/2); no integer rounding or generic Blast ruling is invented. Frag Grenade retains its pinned floor policy. Conditional bonuses, target HP and conditions remain manual.
