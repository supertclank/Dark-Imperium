# Approved Frag Grenade Ruling

For the canonical Frag Grenade:

## Failed Dexterity Save

Take:

`3d6 piercing damage`

## Successful Dexterity Save

Take:

`half of the rolled 3d6 piercing damage, rounded down`

## Ability Modifier

Do not add an ability modifier to Frag Grenade damage.

## Area

15-ft radius.

## Save

Use the existing canonical grenade save DC policy.

## Consumption

The grenade remains expended after use according to the existing grenade rules.

This ruling is specific to the Frag Grenade profile.

It does NOT establish a generic rule that:

- all Blast effects deal half damage on success
- all area weapons deal half damage on success
- all grenades deal half damage on success

Other unresolved Blast/save effects must retain their current blocked state unless their own source text explicitly defines the result.
